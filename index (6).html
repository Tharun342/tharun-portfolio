Machine Learning

Credit Card Fraud Detection

Problem: Fraudulent transactions are difficult to identify because they are rare compared with legitimate transactions. What I did: Prepared data, worked on feature engineering, used SMOTE to address class imbalance, and trained/evaluated a classifier with scikit-learn and LightGBM. The project also explored configuration, logging, error handling, and a simple interface for testing predictions. Project value: Demonstrates an end-to-end classification workflow and why precision and recall matter when false alarms and missed fraud both have costs.
